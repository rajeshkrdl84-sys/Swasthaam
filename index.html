What is Ayurveda?

Ayur = Life. Veda = Knowledge.

Ayurveda is the world's oldest complete medical system — documented in the Charaka Samhita (400 BCE) and rooted in Vedic knowledge going back 5,00,000+ years. Its core principle: disease begins when you live against your own nature. The cure is returning to it.

Charaka Samhita, Sutrasthana Ch.1 · WHO Traditional Medicine Strategy 2025

Your Prakriti — Your Blueprint

The ratio of Vāta, Pitta, Kapha in your body at birth = your Prakriti. It never changes. Health problems arise when your current state moves away from this blueprint. Svastha helps you find it — and return to it.

The Three Forces of Existence

Tri-Guna

Beyond the body — the three qualities that govern all of consciousness, matter, and the cosmos itself

☀
Sattva
Purity · Clarity · Light

The healing state. When Sattva dominates — the body repairs, the mind clarifies, and true health emerges naturally.

🔥
Rajas
Activity · Passion · Motion

The driving force. Rajas initiates change — but without Sattva, it becomes the source of disease through restlessness.

🌑
Tamas
Inertia · Darkness · Rest

The resting state. Necessary for sleep and recovery — but in excess, creates stagnation and the root of chronic disease.
